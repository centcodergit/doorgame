Door game is a short browser game about grabbing a key, getting around a few obstacles, and reaching the door before time runs out.

To play click here:

https://centcodergit.github.io/doorgame/

To move use the arrow keys or WASD. Walk over the key to pick it up, then head for the open door. You don't need to press anything to collect the key or go through. Just go close to the key to collect it The opening screen is basically the tutorial,  grab the key and reach the door to start the three rooms. Once you're in, just try to collect the keys and reach the doors before the time runs out, that's it. If it runs out, that room resets automatically.
Movement has a little momentum, so give yourself some space around corners.
Finish all three rooms and stick around for what happens next. Refresh the page whenever you want to start over.

What's in the game

- A playable intro and three timed rooms with different layouts.
- Sliding movement, circular obstacles, and a clock that keeps things moving.
- Animated characters, doors, and keys, with footsteps and other sound effects.
- A rewind and replay of your successful run, complete with a trail of where you walked.


If you want to run it locally you can do so by:

Just download the files and double press index.html . It's that easy!

How it works

The game uses HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with Canvas drawing the characters and rooms. keys.js tracks the movement from your keyboard, like the WASD and the arrow keys game.js does the movement, collisions, timers, drawing, and the ending. The images and sounds are in their own folders
